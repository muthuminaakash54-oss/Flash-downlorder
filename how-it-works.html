HOW IT WORKS

Three steps.
That's it.

Flash Downloader separates the simple front-end experience from the media-processing service that actually retrieves and converts authorized content.

01

Paste

Copy a supported video page URL and paste it into the downloader.

02

Choose

Select MP4 for video or MP3 for audio where your processing service supports it.

03

Process & save

Your server/API processes the request and returns a download file or approved download URL.

Developer note

A browser-only HTML/CSS/JS site cannot reliably fetch and convert YouTube, Facebook or TikTok media by itself because those services use cross-origin restrictions and dynamic delivery. Connect the supplied front end to your own authorized backend/API using API_ENDPOINT in app.js.
